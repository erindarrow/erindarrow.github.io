Erin Darrow writes fantasy and science fiction inspired by nature, fairy tales, and folklore. When not writing, she wanders the woods, watches birds, cares for cats, and curates an herbal tea collection. Once upon a time, she was a scientist, mapmaker, and recordkeeper in ecology and geology. She lives along the banks of the Waikato river in Aotearoa New Zealand with her partner and calico cat.
<br><br>
Her stories appear in <i>DreamForge Magazine</i>, <i>Metaphorosis Books</i>, and others. You can also find her on <a href="https://www.goodreads.com/erindarrow">GoodReads</a> and <a href="https://bookstodon.com/@darrowerin">Mastodon</a>.
